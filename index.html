The barriers

Four hard truths

Price, coverage, performance, value — Starlink loses on all.

01

Price is 3–8× higher

Jio/Airtel: ₹700–1,600 with OTT. Starlink: ₹2,500–4,200 + ₹30k dish.

02

Fiber & 5G already own cities

Where people live already has fiber or solid 5G. Starlink's edge only matters in empty zones.

03

Latency favours the ground

Fiber: 5–15 ms. Starlink: 20–60 ms. For calls & games, fiber wins when available.

04

No entertainment packs

Jio & Airtel include Netflix, Prime, Hotstar. Starlink is pure bandwidth only.

The rest

Capacity, rules, culture

Even if price matched, structural limits remain.

05

~2 million user ceiling

Practical capacity over India. Jio & Airtel already serve tens of millions.

06

Regulatory friction

Security & FDI clearances still open. Local data rules don't hit Jio/Airtel the same way.

07

India buys on price first

Cheapest reliable plan with a good pack wins. Premium satellite stays niche.
